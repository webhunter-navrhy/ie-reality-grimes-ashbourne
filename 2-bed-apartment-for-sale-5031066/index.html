Grimes are delighted to welcome No. 195 Churchfields to the market. This spacious two bedroom, two bathroom apartment extends to approximately 80 sq. m. / 861 sq. ft. and offers well proportioned accommodation throughout. On entering the property, you are greeted by a welcoming entrance hall. The bright and spacious living room is dual aspect and features laminate wood flooring, with sliding patio doors opening onto a south facing balcony overlooking a green area.

Just off the living room is the kitchen/diner, which is fitted with a modern kitchen incorporating an electric oven and hob. The room is finished with tiled flooring and a tiled splashback surround. The property offers two well proportioned bedrooms, both featuring built in wardrobes and laminate wood flooring. The master bedroom benefits from a fully tiled ensuite bathroom with a walk in shower. The main bathroom is finished with tiled flooring and a tiled bath surround and features a jacuzzi bath with shower over. A separate storage room completes the accommodation. The property further benefits from gas fired central heating, a south facing balcony, and communal parking to the front. Churchfields is ideally located within walking distance of Ashbourne Town Centre and the many amenities the town has to offer, including shops, cafs, restaurants, schools and leisure facilities. The property also benefits from excellent transport links, making it an ideal choice for first time buyers, investors or those seeking a well located home close to all amenities.
